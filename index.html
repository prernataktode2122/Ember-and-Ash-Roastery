How a batch happens

From green to gone-in-a-week

  1. 01

    Cup the greens

    Every incoming lot is cupped blind against last season's sample before we commit a single kilo of shelf space to it.

  2. 02

    Roast in small drums

    12kg per batch on our 1962 drum roaster, tracked by bean color and first-crack timing, not by a fixed profile.

  3. 03

    Rest four days

    Beans de-gas on open trays before bagging — any earlier and the shot pulls sour, any later and it goes flat.

  4. 04

    Bag and date

    One-way valve bags, hand-labelled with the roast date — we'd rather you know it's three weeks old than pretend otherwise.
